1 Fit the wheel drag

Drag the fresh tire from the rack onto the empty hub.

Locked. The car isn't in yet.
Jack locked
Interaction spec
Conceptual action
Place: carry a fresh tire to the axle and seat it.
Physical input
Drag and drop. No tapping. (Keyboard: arrows, Enter.)
Intended feeling
Heavy, then locked in: a real wheel with weight that the hub helps into place.
Affordance / signifier
Tire sits in a dashed rack with grip ticks on its tread. The hub is an empty dashed ring with a small socket at its center.
Feedback + state change
The tire trails slightly behind your hand, like it has weight. Inside the pull zone it slows and drifts toward the hub while the ring thickens. Inside the drop zone the ring goes heavy and solid. Release there: the wheel drops onto the studs with a small bounce, then the ring fills and a check appears. Release elsewhere: the tire drops back to the rack.
What it enables
The hub is filled and the tire is locked, so the jack can be dropped.

2 Drop the jack swipe

Flick down on the car. Light all three chevrons to drop it.

Locked. Fit the tire first.
Release locked
Interaction spec
Conceptual action
Lower: take the car off the jack and put its weight back on the ground.
Physical input
A downward swipe, judged on both distance and speed. (Keyboard: Enter.)
Intended feeling
Snappy and decisive. The jack resists hesitation.
Affordance / signifier
Three stacked down chevrons, dim and dashed. They light from the top down as you swipe faster, and all three lit means fast enough. Hollow dashed car and jack mean locked.
Feedback + state change
The car follows your finger down while you swipe and the chevrons light with live speed. Fast and far enough: the car drops and the jack collapses. Too slow or too short: the jack catches the car and it jolts back up past its start, then settles.
What it enables
The car is on the ground and the jack is flat, so the release lever unlocks.

3 Send the car pull

Pull the lever past the line, then let go.

Locked
Locked. Lower the car first.
Car held
Interaction spec
Conceptual action
Commit: send the car out. This is the one irreversible step.
Physical input
Drag the lever up past a commit line, then release. (Keyboard: Up arrow, Enter.)
Intended feeling
Weight and finality: nothing moves until the lever slams.
Affordance / signifier
Dashed, hollow lever labeled Locked means unavailable. Solid lever with an up arrow and the word Pull means ready. The dashed line shows how far is far enough.
Feedback + state change
The lever lags behind your hand, so it feels heavy, and the track fills behind it. Crossing the line inverts the lever and the label changes to Let go. Releasing early lets it drop back under its own weight with a small bounce. Releasing past the line slams the lever to the top, stops the clock, and only then launches the car.
What it enables
The car is gone and the lever goes inert. The drill ends with your result.
